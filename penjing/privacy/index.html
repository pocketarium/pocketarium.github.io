Privacy Policy

Penjing《枝》 · Effective November 25, 2026

Studio Penjing Privacy

Pocketarium builds Penjing《枝》 with privacy by design. We do not sell your personal data, operate user-tracking accounts, or collect personal analytics.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Your puzzle solutions, seal scores, collection shelf, painting manual entries, hint balance, and audio/haptic/language preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Platform Cloud Saves & Achievements

When you sign in to Apple Game Center / iCloud (iOS) or Google Play Games Services (Android), your game progress and achievements may be synced through Apple's or Google's platform services so you can restore your progress across devices on the same platform. Pocketarium does not run its own game-save servers or receive your Apple/Google account credentials.

3. Purchases & Optional Rewarded Ads

  • In-App Purchases: Full Edition unlocks and purchase restorations are processed directly by Apple App Store or Google Play. We only verify entitlement status provided by the store.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: Where rewarded video ads are offered (such as unlocking a chapter gate or earning extra hints), watching an ad is strictly player-initiated. Any advertising SDK integrated in a release may process device identifiers or ad-delivery signals in accordance with its own privacy policy and your OS consent / tracking settings.

4. Support Emails

If you email us at pocketarium+penjing@gmail.com, we use your message and email address solely to reply to your support request.
