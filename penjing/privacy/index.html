Privacy Policy

Penjing《枝》 · Effective November 26, 2026

Studio Penjing Privacy

Pocketarium builds Penjing《枝》 with privacy by design. On iOS, the app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no ads, and no cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal data, require account registration, or collect personal analytics.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Your puzzle solutions, seal scores, collection shelf, painting manual entries, hint balance, and audio/haptic/language preferences are stored locally on your device so you can play offline.

Deletion & Reset: Open Settings (the seal icon on the home screen) and tap Reset progress → Reset device to erase local progress at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all local app data.

2. Platform Cloud Saves & Achievements

When you are signed in to Apple iCloud / Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games Services (Android), your game progress and unlocked achievements may sync through Apple's or Google's platform services so you can restore your progress across devices on the same platform. Pocketarium does not operate separate game-save servers and never receives your Apple Account or Google credentials.

Deletion & Opt-Out: You can disable iCloud sync for Penjing in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud, or choose Reset progress → Reset device & cloud in the game's Settings to clear synced cloud progress.

3. Purchases & Platform Policy

  • StoreKit / In-App Purchases: The one-time Full Edition purchase and purchase restorations are processed directly by the Apple App Store (StoreKit 2) or Google Play. Pocketarium only checks your store entitlement status to unlock chapters and unlimited hints, and never sees your payment or billing details. Your Full Edition purchase entitlement remains preserved by the store even if you reset game progress.
  • Zero Ads & No Tracking (iOS): The iOS version of Penjing《枝》 integrates no third-party advertising SDKs and performs no cross-app tracking (NSPrivacyTracking = false). On Android releases where optional, player-initiated rewarded ads may be offered, an ad is loaded only when you choose to watch one and operates under your OS consent settings.

4. Support Emails

If you email us at pocketarium+penjing@gmail.com, we use your message and email address solely to reply to your support request.
