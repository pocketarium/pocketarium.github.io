Clip one twig, let the springs pass, and grow the tree into its shape.

Inspired by the traditional Lingnan art of pruning and branch training, Penjing《枝》 unfolds across 9 chapters and 175 puzzles—introducing elm, plum, scholars' rock, window light, pine, deadwood, grove plantings, curio-shelf frames, and banyan aerial roots.

Support & FAQ

Need help with cloud saves, restoring your Full Edition purchase, or reporting a bug? Reach us at pocketarium+penjing@gmail.com. Including your device model, OS version, and language setting helps us respond faster.

  • Restoring Purchases: Open Settings (the seal icon on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account or Google account used for the original purchase.
  • Cloud Saves: Progress syncs within the same platform ecosystem (iOS via iCloud / Apple GameSave; Android via Google Play Games Services) when enabled and signed in.
  • Offline Play: All unlocked puzzles, the painting manual (codex), and your collection shelf remain playable offline.
